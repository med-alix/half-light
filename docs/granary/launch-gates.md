# Launch gates

NXN and the Granary launch **no earlier than 16 March 2027** (the XEN APY floor), and only if all four gates pass. If one fails, launch moves to the next 90-day step.

1. **The game holds players without a token.** Season 1 day-30 retention of at least 25% with no token in the game.
2. **Clean external audit** of NXN, the Granary, the vaults and the escrow, with no open high-severity findings.
3. **Community review.** The DBXen and NXD communities have seen the routing, the deposit rules and the release rule.
4. **Legal review** in the main player jurisdictions, including the territory rewards and sponsor mechanics.

Why this order: the token should amplify a game people already enjoy, not replace one. If the game cannot keep people without NXN, NXN would only be another farm.
