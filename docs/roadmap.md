# Roadmap

Every phase starts on a date of XEN's own calendar, and only when the previous phase is ready. If a phase is not ready, it moves to the next 90-day step.

| Phase | Anchor date | What happens | Gate to move on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open design** | Now | Community review of this book; measure ε, burn and mint rates on chain; fit the model; run the missing simulations | Rough agreement on the [open questions](open-questions.md) marked for Season 1 |
| **Pre-season** | The Half-Light, 16 Nov 2026, or later | Read-only prototype: sky, lineage cards, live sun, tribe join | Working indexer, players signing in |
| **Season 1** | 16 Dec 2026 or 16 Mar 2027 | Map, daily turns, war-burns, rescues, the first Reaping. No token. | Day-30 retention ≥ 25% |
| **Audit and review** | In parallel with Season 1 | NXN, Granary, vaults and escrow written, audited, reviewed by the DBXen and NXD communities, legal review | All [launch gates](granary/launch-gates.md) pass |
| **NXN launch** | Earliest 16 Mar 2027 | Granary goes live; Hearth, treasuries and NXN prices switch on in the game | — |
| **Seasons** | Every 90 days | New content, temples, realms on other chains | — |
| **The Last Light** | ~24 Dec 2030 | AMP reaches 1; the Granary starts releasing; Twilight begins | — |
| **The Long Fire** | ~Apr 2032 | The map is sized by the Granary store | — |

## Who is needed

Front-end and indexer developers, a Solidity developer and auditors, game designers, modellers to fit the coefficients, artists and writers for lore. The project lead's role: game design, models, simulations and trade-offs, using AI-assisted coding.
