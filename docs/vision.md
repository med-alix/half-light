# Vision

XEN, DBXen, NXD and Fenix already behave like games: rank races, daily prize pools, decay, penalties and survivors who inherit from the fallen. What they lack is a stadium, a scoreboard and a story. HALF-LIGHT is that layer, and the NXN protocol is the piece of economics the stack is missing.

## What HALF-LIGHT is

A slow, diplomacy-driven survival strategy game whose physics are the hyperstructure's own contract math. One turn is one DBXen cycle (24 hours). One season is one XEN APY step (90 days). It is asynchronous and suits holders, not twitch gamers: think Diplomacy, Neptune's Pride or Dark Forest, on a world that is literally cooling.

The name comes from the calendar. On **16 November 2026**, AMP crosses 1,500: the sun is at half its original light.

## Design pillars

| Pillar | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Protocol is physics | Every world law is anchored to a verified contract rule. The game reads the chain and never invents yield. |
| Time and participation over capital | XEN's mint reward ignores balance, so in-game power does too. Size is always dampened with a square root or a log, and capped. |
| Zero extra cost alignment | The core actions are protocol actions holders already take: mint, claim, burn, stake. The game gives them meaning and coordination. |
| Self-custody, no house | The game never holds assets or runs a pot. Rewards are protocol-native or non-financial (titles, territory, cosmetics), plus NXN emission that already exists. |
| The hierarchy is the map | XEN is the surface, burners are the temples, DBXen is the forge, NXD is the core, Fenix is rebirth, the Granary is the Hearth. |
| Open by design | The rules engine is open source and deterministic. Anyone can recompute the game from chain events. Content is data, so contributors and AI tools can add regions, lore and art without touching the rules. |

## What it brings to each protocol

| Protocol | What it gains |
| --- | --- |
| XEN | Daily reasons to mint, claim on time and stake; rescues that save holders from late-claim penalties |
| DBXen | Coordinated burns from day one (war-burns, sponsor crates), and a winter reserve of XEN after the floor |
| NXD | More DBXen fees for its staked DXN; a seat in the world as the Core |
| Burner projects | A discovery channel as temples on the map |

## The long view

The game is built to last as long as XEN's clock: through the Creation era to 2030, the Twilight to about 2032, and the Long Fire after that. A 3D third-person world gives it presence, but the real game state is a small, open, verifiable 2D war table. That split keeps the game cheap to build now and open to whatever tools, contributors and AI characters come later.
