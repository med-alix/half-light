# The Reaping

![The Reaping coliseum](../assets/art/reaping.jpg)

The last 10 days of every season.

## The draw

A future block hash, committed at least 64 blocks ahead and published before the draw, picks **2 tributes per tribe** from active members, plus **1 Outlaw wildcard**. Their names go on the board.

## The arena

Tributes play 10 daily rounds on a small frozen map. Each round they choose: **move, hide, forage or fight**. The safe zone shrinks every round. Season 1 keeps these choices simple; more skill can come later if players want it.

## Sponsor crates

Anyone can sponsor any tribute.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Cost | Burn XEN in any temple (a DBXen burn counts, proven by its on-chain event), or burn 1 NXN |
| Contents (game-only) | Warmth for a cold round, a shield against one elimination, a scout reveal, a lure that pulls a rival toward the edge |
| Limit | Crates per sponsor, per tribute, per day = min(3, ⌊√batches⌋); each crate weaker than the last |
| Sponsor gets | Their name in the tribute's chronicle, and a permanent Patron title if the tribute wins |

Crates are in-game only and non-transferable. Nobody is paid by another player: every crate is a burn. The audience becomes burners, and a tribute in trouble pulls in a wave of DBXen burns.

## The victor

The last tribute standing wins a permanent title, a seat on the Hearth Council, and a warmth bonus for their tribe's home sector next season. Their tribe's prestige counts toward the season bonus (see [Winning, losing and NXN](winning-and-nxn.md)).
